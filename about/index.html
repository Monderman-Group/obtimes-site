About the journal

Current events.
Enduring questions.

Independent essays with evidence, intellectual depth and room to think.

What we examine

On Borrowed Times looks at how institutions work, how they drift, and how people and technologies change them. Its essays connect immediate events with questions that last: trust, judgment, responsibility, knowledge and the systems we rely on.

We welcome clear arguments and serious disagreement. The aim is to understand a question more fully, follow the evidence and make the reasoning visible.

Open to readers. Open to ideas.

Every essay is freely accessible. Optional email updates let readers request new work without a paywall.

New and established writers are welcome to offer essays for private editorial consideration. Research, fairness and a willingness to be edited matter more than a particular institutional affiliation.
